Word of the Day Entry #4829

浪漫

long6 maan6

Romantic; an transliteration of the English word "romantic", capturing both the sound and the spirit of drifting waves.

Source: Modern Cantonese Lexicon north_east
靚
leng3

Beautiful / Good

The quintessential Cantonese descriptor for aesthetic excellence and quality.

攰
gui6

Tired / Exhausted

Specifically used to describe physical fatigue after a long day's work.

飲
jam2

To Drink

Often used in "jam2 caa4" (dim sum) to signify a social gathering.
